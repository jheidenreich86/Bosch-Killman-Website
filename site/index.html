Who We Are

A Century of West Michigan Law

Grand Rapids, since 1910.

Bosch Killman VanderWal is a full-service firm of nine attorneys serving the region's businesses and families. The core of our practice is insurance defense — no-fault, auto negligence, and premises liability matters for carriers and businesses statewide — surrounded by a full-service bench: business, litigation, estate plans, real estate, and family law. Our attorneys bring the judgment that only a century of West Michigan matters can build — with the responsiveness of a firm where you always know your lawyer.
